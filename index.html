Why this exists

“The best people are being pushed aside because they were born in the wrong time and place. No one is listening.”

The Green Tank creates another door: no prestige test, no wealth test, and no requirement to speak the language of power. A claim still has to survive evidence, safety and public scrutiny—but the person bringing it deserves to be heard.

The open test standard

No promise becomes policy without a way to prove it.

We meter inputs, outputs, losses, water, energy, emissions, uptime, safety, costs and defects. We listen to residents and workers. We publish what the result suggests—and what it does not prove.

State the problem

Name who is affected, what is failing and what a better outcome would mean.

Separate the claims

Mark evidence, interpretation, projection and ambition so they cannot be confused.

Design the test

Set inputs, outputs, costs, safety limits, decision gates and conditions for stopping.

Run an open pilot

Involve affected people and independent specialists. Record defects and honest failures.

Publish and decide

Release results in plain language. Improve, scale or stop according to the evidence.

Every project will show

A public evidence record

Sources

Where each factual claim came from.

Method

How data was cleaned, compared or tested.

Costs

Purchase, installation, operation and whole-life estimates.

Limits

Unknowns, conflicts, risks and what is excluded.

Results

Measurements, user experience and independent review.

Decision

Why the work should improve, scale, pause or stop.
