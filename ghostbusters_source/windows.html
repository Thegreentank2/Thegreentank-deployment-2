The Many Windows

A single window can show something true without showing the whole landscape.

Human

Spiritual

Future

Unknown

What have we not discovered, measured or imagined yet?

Many views do not automatically create truth. They create a better chance of finding it when we compare observations, preserve disagreement and check evidence.

The question that protects this room is simple: What am I not seeing?

This is an independent reflective project and is not affiliated with the Ghostbusters film franchise.

Evidence, personal experience and interpretation are kept separate where possible. Questions are welcome.

AL's spiritual interpretation

AL uses “God” as a personal philosophical/spiritual way of talking about love, care, relationship, cohesion and existence, and “Buddha” as a symbol and tradition of awakening, compassion and learning to see more clearly.

This is intentionally kept separate from scientific claims. Physics and mathematics can reveal relationships and structures in nature; deciding that those structures should be called “God” is a metaphysical interpretation rather than an experimentally established result.

No visitor needs to use the same language to participate in the practical lesson.
