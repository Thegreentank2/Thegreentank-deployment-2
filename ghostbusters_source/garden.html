The Garden

No throne at the end of the path.

A garden is not a factory

A factory can aim for identical outputs. A garden depends on difference, relationship, time and conditions. Growth cannot be forced by pulling on the plant.

The goal is not to become the greatest tree. Grow well, and allow other things to grow too.

No one is more human

People differ in ability, experience, culture, confidence, knowledge and need. Those differences do not make one person more human than another.

Equality does not require identical treatment in every situation. Sometimes equal dignity requires different support.

What understanding asks of us

Understanding is not the end of responsibility. It is where responsibility becomes more informed.

What did I learn?What did I question?What can I repair?What can I share?

Continue

You do not need to become AL. You do not need to find the same answers. You only need enough room to keep looking honestly.

We grow because we are unfinished. We understand because we listen. We reduce harm when care and evidence work together.

This is an independent reflective project and is not affiliated with the Ghostbusters film franchise.

Evidence, personal experience and interpretation are kept separate where possible. Questions are welcome.
