The Signal — Project 67

Sometimes words are not enough.

A message looking for many voices

Project 67 asks whether a message can remain connected while its form changes across language, genre, culture and personal experience.

A world of identical voices would not be harmony. Harmony depends on difference held in relationship.

The river

A river has a source. The source matters. But a river forbidden to leave its source never becomes a river. It meets other streams, landscapes and living systems as it travels.

This became a way to think about authorship and sharing: remember where something began without claiming ownership over every meaning it later creates for other people.

The lesson

A signal offers itself. It does not force the receiver. The listener brings their own memory, culture and emotion. Communication is a relationship, not control.

This is an independent reflective project and is not affiliated with the Ghostbusters film franchise.

Evidence, personal experience and interpretation are kept separate where possible. Questions are welcome.
