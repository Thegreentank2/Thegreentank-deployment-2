Ghostbusters

A True Story?

A journey through fear, imagination, learning and connection.

Not a story about a hero. A story about a person who became lost, suffered, searched, made mistakes, built questions, and slowly learned to look again.

Sometimes the monster is real. Sometimes it is partly real. Sometimes fear finishes drawing it for us. The lesson is not to shame fear, but to look carefully enough to know the difference.

Three layers of honesty

Documented

Repositories, public pages, code, dates, media and other things a visitor can inspect.

Experienced

What AL remembers, felt and understood from living through the journey.

Interpreted

Spiritual, philosophical or symbolic meaning drawn from experience. Offered as a lens, not imposed as scientific proof.

Unknown

Things we do not yet know. They remain visible because an honest empty space is better than a confident invention.

This is an independent reflective project and is not affiliated with the Ghostbusters film franchise.

Evidence, personal experience and interpretation are kept separate where possible. Questions are welcome.
