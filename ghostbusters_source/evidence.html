The Evidence Room

Not a trophy cabinet. A set of questions, experiments, mistakes and public records.

How to read this room

Repository links let visitors inspect the work directly. A repository shows that material was created and published; it does not automatically prove every conclusion written inside it.

Proof of Experience Crypto

Question: Can human experience itself have value?

Experiment: A conceptual alternative to machine- or capital-centred validation, initially through creative participation.

Lesson: Human beings are more than labour, computing power or purchasing power.

Unfinished: It remains a concept, not a production cryptocurrency.

What these records do not prove

They do not by themselves establish who was influenced by the work, why another person behaved as they did, that later cultural events were caused by Project 67, or that a spiritual interpretation is a scientific result. Those questions remain separate.

This is an independent reflective project and is not affiliated with the Ghostbusters film franchise.

Evidence, personal experience and interpretation are kept separate where possible. Questions are welcome.

Public-source safety note
