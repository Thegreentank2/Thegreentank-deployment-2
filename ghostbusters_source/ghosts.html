The Ghosts

The ghosts are not enemies. They are useful signals that can become distorted when they operate with incomplete information.

Fear — the alarm

Fear asks: “Could this hurt me?” That is useful. The ghost begins when fear changes the question into: “How can I prove this will hurt me?”

Fear can protect life. It can also carry old danger into a new moment. The feeling may be completely real while the explanation remains incomplete.

Unknown→Alarm→Story→Reaction

Ego — the protector and storyteller

The ego says: “I have a view.” The ghost says: “My view is the whole world.”

Ego gives identity, confidence, memory, creativity and protection. It becomes dangerous when image matters more than evidence, or when being wrong feels like annihilation.

A healthy ego can say: “I thought I understood. I was wrong. Let me learn.”

Perfection — the beautiful prison

Growth says: “I want to improve.” Fear says: “I must become flawless before I deserve acceptance.”

Perfection can be an illusion of grandeur built from fear of rejection. A being that already knows everything would have nothing new to discover, nothing to relate to and no learning left. Learning is not evidence of failure. It is one of the joys of being unfinished.

Assumption — the gap filler

A guess can help us move quickly. A guess pretending to be a fact can create a monster.

The mind dislikes missing information and naturally completes patterns. The skill is not to stop imagining. It is to mark the boundary between what I saw, what I inferred, and what remains unknown.

How monsters are manifested

Here, “manifesting a monster” means constructing a threatening interpretation from incomplete information and then reacting as though every part were already known.

Something unclear→Fear reacts→Ego explains→Assumption fills gaps→Monster

Important: this does not mean all danger is imaginary. Some dangers are real. The purpose is to distinguish observed danger from the parts imagination added.

This is an independent reflective project and is not affiliated with the Ghostbusters film franchise.

Evidence, personal experience and interpretation are kept separate where possible. Questions are welcome.
