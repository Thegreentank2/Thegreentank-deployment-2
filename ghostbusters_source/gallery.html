Small memes for difficult moments

Fear: “Something changed!”
Wisdom: “Thank you. We will look.”
Perfection: “Don't start until you can do it flawlessly.”
Learning: “That would make starting impossible.”
“My perspective is true.”
Good. Now show me the other windows.

Why humour belongs here

Humour can reduce the shame attached to being wrong. The purpose is never to laugh at suffering. It is to make correction survivable enough that learning can continue.

Visual explanations supplement rather than replace the story and evidence.
