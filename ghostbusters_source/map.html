The four distinctions

ObservedWhat was actually seen or recorded.
InferredThe explanation we currently think fits.
FeltThe lived emotional or bodily experience.
UnknownWhat remains open.

The principle

No single medium, person, discipline or belief is required to contain the entire answer. Compare views, keep the evidence visible, and allow learning to change the model.
