# Freedom Vision — interactive static prototype v0.6

This is a *functional, static prototype* for The Green Tank; it is **not deployed**. Open `index.html` locally, or serve its folder using a static HTTP server such as `python3 -m http.server 8080` and visit `http://localhost:8080`.

## Design
- One public search interface across selected archive APIs.
- Watch, Listen, Read, World News.
- Classic cinema, vintage cartoons, classic television, music and books quick collections.
- Live search requests only after a user clicks Search or a collection; no background crawling, accounts, adverts, analytics, autoplay or video thumbnails.
- Compact, responsive layout; mobile-friendly and keyboard accessible.
- Original source links and direct official donation links only (currently Internet Archive).

## What works today in the prototype
- Internet Archive Advanced Search API (manual, low-result metadata query) for any subject and category, including collections: feature_films, animationandcartoons, classic_tv.
- Wikimedia Commons cross-site MediaWiki Action API search for video (Watch tab with search terms).
- Project Gutenberg ebook catalogue search through third-party Gutendex (Read tab).
- LibriVox audiobook search through its API (Listen tab), with the source's latest spacing and request limits respected.
- Source index with additional providers **as external links only** (not embedded): Blender Open Movies, PeerTube, Library of Congress, BFI Replay, National Film Board of Canada, Audius, Funkwhale, Jamendo, ccMixter, Dogmazic, Open Library, OAPEN, DOAB, Europeana and NASA.
- In-page video/audio/PDF only for an Internet Archive item that has an open-licence / public-domain indication **in archive metadata** AND already has a file with verified low-bandwidth properties. It never triggers remote conversion or rescales high-resolution video.
- Video must be <=480px tall, <=960px wide, <=1.25Mbps measured from file size and duration; audio <=160kbps; PDF <=10MB. If any field is missing, fall back to the archive link without loading the media. Rights still need independent jurisdiction-specific verification.
- Rate limits: one search per provider per several seconds, no retries on 429/503/timeouts, source-specific cool-down periods, seven-minute in-memory metadata cache.

## Limitations and safeguards
- Cross-origin API availability and actual media CORS have *not* been verified in the hosting environment. Browser failures are surfaced with source links, without retries. This is a working proof-of-concept, not a guaranteed production service.
- Site-wide server rate limiting cannot be enforced by a static page; it would need a small controlled search gateway or providers' own published quota mechanisms.
- BFI Replay, NFB, PeerTube, Audius, Funkwhale, Jamendo, etc. are **discovery only** pending API, content, advert and rights review. Do not mislabel them as connected or verified ad-free embedded players.
- No YouTube or Mixcloud, including hidden embed frames.
- No live news channels are embedded. International news broadcasters require individual access, advertisements, local licensing and resolution checks.
- Free-to-watch != public-domain. Existing Archive metadata is not a conclusive rights determination; restored copies, soundtracks and territorial copyright may differ. In production, use a validated allowlist of individual playable files before exposing an in-site player publicly.
- No public promotion of donating to Freedom Vision. If a verified donation page exists it must belong to the archive and be linked directly, never processed or monetized by Freedom Vision.
- The official Green Tank development website cannot be edited using this repository connection. Its GitHub Pages mirror is generated by a pinned, integrity-checked deployment workflow; do not replace that workflow's generated files with this prototype without reviewing and testing integration.

## Culture & Languages and World Cinema (draft extension)

Two additional front-page entrances and navigation tabs are included in the draft:
- **Culture & Languages**: curated external, official links to LanguageGuide.org, OpenLearn, TV5MONDE Apprendre, NHK Easy Japanese, LibreLingo, Tatoeba and Lingua Libre. These are discovery links only. They are not treated as automatically reusable course content. Tatoeba text and audio permissions vary by record; LibreLingo's code and course-content licences differ.
- **World Cinema**: curated external, official links to FIAF's free-film archive directory, Japan Foundation JFF Theater, National Film Archive of Japan's Animated Film Classics, Korean Film Archive KMDb VOD, Chinese Film Classics educational project, and Hong Kong Film Archive. Individual English-subtitle, availability, payment, advertising, regional rights and account requirements must be checked.

**No YouTube or Mixcloud players are integrated.** Chinese Film Classics is included as an academic reference only; most films there are delivered via YouTube, so do not display or embed them inside Freedom Vision. JFF Theater requires its own free account and country-specific access. Japanese Animated Film Classics explicitly prohibits video embedding: link to the official archive, never its stream.

**Stephen Chow:** modern Hong Kong films (for example, *A Chinese Odyssey* (1995), *Shaolin Soccer* (2001), *Kung Fu Hustle* (2004)) are not assumed public domain. Hong Kong Film Archive's free-to-browse screening information (including English-subtitled showings) does not authorise digital playback. No Stephen Chow films are enabled for in-page playback without a verified, legally authorised and ad-free provider.

Future learning options: region and language filter; English subtitles when supplied by the provider; per-item attribution; authorised CC0/CC-BY language sentence samples; user-controlled manual audio replay; and a low-data plain-text study mode. Avoid automatic subtitle downloads, speculative rights claims, unauthorised translations, or mirrored copyrighted language lessons.

## Science, mathematics and free audio lessons (draft extension)

New navigation entries and collection cards: **Science & Maths** and **Audio Lessons**.

- **Curated discovery only** until each specific provider's player, rights, low-bandwidth alternative, attribution and API/embedding conditions are validated. The sources are official external links, not embedded university lectures or mirrored audio.
- Science directory: MIT OpenCourseWare Quantum Physics I; Yale physics I and II (including quantum), organic chemistry and evolution/biology lectures; CERN educational physics videos; Perimeter Institute PIRSA seminars; PhET simulations; GeoGebra geometry; OpenStax texts; OpenLearn science and maths; NASA podcasts.
- Audio directory: Language Transfer free audio course downloads, NHK Easy Japanese, Yale lecture MP3s, OpenLearn audio resources and NASA official podcasts. YouTube, Mixcloud and SoundCloud embedded players remain **excluded**, even when a resource uses them as an optional playback method.
- Only local provider-name/description filtering is offered for these new directories. Searching a phrase does **not** query MIT, Yale, CERN or other third-party providers. No media preloads, thumbnails, site crawling or automatic playback.
- Open Yale Courses provides audio-only MP3, low-bandwidth MOV and transcripts on individual session pages. Reuse is generally Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 3.0; individual third-party material may be excluded.
- MIT OCW has downloadable videos and course notes. Its Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms require noncommercial use, attribution and ShareAlike; file-level checks still needed.
- PhET has official embed codes but its present CC BY-NC 4.0 licence and website terms require non-commercial use and attribution. Do not embed by default.
- GeoGebra's web services have separate non-commercial licensing terms. External links only until cleared.
- OpenStax textbooks can be read free online and as PDFs; check each exact title's current licence and credits rather than assuming blanket reuse.
- NASA podcasts provide direct links, including RSS, but any optional SoundCloud destination is not used inside Freedom Vision.
- Audio streams will be subject to the same user-controlled start/stop, provider rate limits, attribution, no advertising and direct-provider-support rules as other media.
